# SnapList illustrated camera mascot system

## Current authority

Aziz approved the illustrated instant-camera concept family on 2026-07-15 for
use in Claude Design mockups. The complete raster visual master is:

`source/camera-mascot-approved-concept-sheet.png`

Four transparent RGBA state sheets are supplied in `states/`, and twelve
transparent individual pose crops are supplied under `poses/` for direct
placement. They are not separate characters; treat them as states of the same
camera guide. Use the individual pose files in mockups rather than placing an
entire multi-pose sheet. Do not redraw, vectorize, recolor, regenerate, or
replace the character during this design pass. Production vectorization remains
a later gate after placements and sizes are approved.

## Visual contract

- friendly illustrated instant camera;
- white body, near-black base/limbs, cool-gray detail, restrained cobalt blue;
- bold outlined 2D editorial/clip-art-like treatment with limited shading;
- one subtle expressive lens, never a large pasted-on human face;
- playful and useful without becoming childish or game-like;
- the same silhouette and palette across all states.

## Supplied state mapping

| File | Meaning | Good placements |
|---|---|---|
| `states/01-coaching-and-result.png` | explain, show a photo, guide the next action | outcome promise, capture/photo primer, first usable result |
| `states/02-analyzing-and-review.png` | inspect, compare, review evidence | real processing status, comp review, identity check |
| `states/03-uncertainty-and-retry.png` | question, missing evidence, recoverable problem | low confidence, denied permission, interrupted upload, retry |
| `states/04-quiet-success.png` | confirmed completion or reassurance | preserved work, draft ready, publish success, restored purchase |

Claude Design may non-destructively crop a pose already present in these files.
Do not invent a new pose or combine limbs/faces from different crops.

## Placement rules

- Use the mascot only when it clarifies a first-use, system, or recovery moment.
- Keep it out of routine listing rows, dense draft fields, buyer conversations,
  analytics charts, and normal account settings.
- Never let it compete with the item photo, price, evidence, publish action,
  buyer message, or required order action.
- One character appearance per screen is normally enough.
- Product meaning must remain complete in adjacent text; expression and color
  are never the sole status signal.

Suggested mockup sizes:

- compact guide/avatar: 28–44 pt;
- inline helper: 56–88 pt;
- state illustration: 96–144 pt;
- onboarding hero: 144–196 pt.

## Motion intent

Motion is optional and functional: small lens focus, one brief scan, one
questioning tilt, or one quiet success settle. Bind processing motion to real
backend status. Never loop aggressively, fabricate progress, delay an action,
or use confetti by default. Reduced Motion uses the static crop.

## Transparent mockup assets

The PNGs in this package have true alpha transparency and preserve the full
approved silhouettes, detached blue expression marks, props, and safe crop
margins. Do not run another automatic background-removal pass in Claude Design.

## Not yet approved for production

The current sheet is a design-stage raster master. After high-fidelity screens
establish exact sizes, export individual high-resolution raster assets or create
a carefully reviewed vector family. Any conversion must match the approved
silhouette, lens expression, palette, outline weight, and proportions.
